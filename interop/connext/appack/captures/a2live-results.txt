A2-live: OUR stack vs LIVE RTI Connext 7.3.1, application acknowledgment (ADR 0090 §3.3)
========================================================================================

THE QUESTION. We emit APP_ACK (submessage 0x1c) and the acknowledgment_kind ParameterId 0x800b under
OUR OWN VendorId (0x01FF), never RTI's -- claiming RTI's would make every other submessage in the
datagram interpretable under RTI's rules and would misstate who sent it. Whether Connext HONOURS
either from a foreign VendorId cannot be settled by reading code. These are the measurements.

Transport: loopback UDPv4 (the harness profile forces it; Connext prefers SHMEM same-host, which
never reaches the wire). Capture: tcpdump -i lo0 (classic pcap, no editcap needed).

LEG 1 - our :application-explicit READER  <-  Connext APPLICATION_EXPLICIT writer
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A2LIVE domain=130 reader-kind=:APPLICATION-EXPLICIT advertising 127.0.0.1
A2LIVE RESULT discovered=1 matched=1 taken=5 acked=5 app-acks-sent-arrivals=0 app-ack-confs-received=5
A2LIVE Q1-match=MATCHED Q2-conf=CONF-RECEIVED

  wire evidence (submessage counted by SENDER vendorId, from the capture itself):
    vendorId 01ff (US)          APP_ACK       x5
    vendorId 0101 (RTI Connext) APP_ACK_CONF  x5
  => Connext ACCEPTED our APP_ACK from a foreign VendorId and CONFIRMED every one.
     RTI confirms only an APP_ACK it accepted, so the confirmation IS the acceptance.

LEG 2 - our :application-auto WRITER  ->  Connext APPLICATION_AUTO reader
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A2LIVEW domain=138 writer-kind=:APPLICATION-AUTO
A2LIVEW discovered=1 matched=1
A2LIVEW wrote=5 stored-after-write=5
A2LIVEW RESULT matched=1 stored-at-end=0 app-acks=2 last-sn=5 app-unacked=0
A2LIVEW VERDICT history RELEASED
[connext-appack-sub] RESULT: took 5 sample(s)
  => Connext's reader app-acked US; our APPLICATION watermark advanced to SN 5 and the
     HistoryCache was RELEASED to 0. A Connext reader cannot stall our writer.

     NOTE this leg first failed to match at all, and the cause was DATA_REPRESENTATION,
     NOT application acknowledgment: make-writer-qos offers XCDR2 only and the Connext
     reader in this configuration accepted XCDR1. Recorded so it is not misread.

LEG 3 (CONTROL) - our :protocol READER  <-  Connext APPLICATION_EXPLICIT writer
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A2LIVE domain=132 reader-kind=:PROTOCOL advertising 127.0.0.1
A2LIVE RESULT discovered=1 matched=0 taken=5 acked=0 app-acks-sent-arrivals=0 app-ack-confs-received=0
A2LIVE Q1-match=NO-MATCH Q2-conf=NO-CONF
  => WE refuse the match (matched=0, REQUESTED_INCOMPATIBLE_QOS naming :acknowledgment-kind).
     CONNEXT MATCHES ANYWAY and delivers all 5 samples. Its writer is then waiting for
     acknowledgments that will never come.

⚠️ AN UNRELATED OBSERVATION FROM LEG 3, NOT AN APP-ACK FINDING, BUT LOUDER THAN ONE
====================================================================================
Leg 3 reports matched=0 AND taken=5. Our reader refused the match on RxO -- and still delivered the
refused writer's samples to the application. The RxO gate decided "incompatible" and the data arrived
anyway, which makes the gate's verdict advisory rather than binding.

Hypothesis (NOT yet confirmed): the WP-N-ENDPOINT-S2 delivery route falls back to the primary reader
when a source GUID has no route -- documented there as the "N=1/pre-match" case -- so an unmatched
remote writer's samples land on the primary. That path predates application acknowledgment; app-ack
merely made it observable, because this is the first QoS whose refusal is expected to be absolute.

It needs its own investigation and is recorded here rather than folded into ADR 0090.

WHAT THIS SETTLES
=================
1. Cross-vendor application acknowledgment WORKS IN BOTH DIRECTIONS under our own VendorId.
   A proposed "vendor gate" -- match an APPLICATION kind only against a proven VendorId -- was
   DROPPED as a result: it would have refused a combination that demonstrably works.

2. RTI DOES NOT REFUSE to match a reader that cannot acknowledge. DDS matching is per-side, so our
   own equality gate cannot stop a peer from matching us: we can be the CAUSE of a foreign writer's
   unbounded retention and cannot prevent it. Our own writer is protected (the gate keeps a
   :protocol reader out of the purge watermark) and our refusal is visible, not silent.

REPRODUCING
===========
  export NDDSHOME=/Applications/rti_connext_dds-7.3.1 CONNEXTDDS_ARCH=arm64Darwin20clang12.0
  # edit USER_QOS_PROFILES.xml for the acknowledgment_kind under test, then per leg:

  ./appack_pub <domain> 5 18       # leg 1/3: RTI writes, our reader acknowledges
  ./appack_sub <domain> 24         # leg 2:   RTI reads, our writer is acknowledged
