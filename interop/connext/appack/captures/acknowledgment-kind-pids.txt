SEDP vendor ParameterIds vs acknowledgment_kind - RTI Connext 7.3.1 (ADR 0090 slice A3b)
================================================================================================

HOW THIS WAS PRODUCED. The capture harness in the parent directory was run THREE TIMES with
exactly one thing changed - acknowledgment_kind in USER_QOS_PROFILES.xml - and the SEDP
publication (writerId 0x000003c2) and subscription (0x000004c2) ParameterLists compared:

    python3 captures/sedpscan.py <capture.pcap>

Loopback UDP was captured with tcpdump (classic pcap, so no editcap step; tshark on macOS
writes pcapng and does not dissect DLT_NULL frames anyway). Only vendor-range PIDs (>= 0x8000)
and PID_RELIABILITY (0x001a) are shown - those are the ones that could plausibly carry the
policy, and RELIABILITY is shown to demonstrate that it does NOT (it stays the standard 12
octets: kind + max_blocking_time, with no room for an extra field).

================================================================================================
THE FINDING
================================================================================================

0x800b is the ONLY field that moves, and it moves on BOTH endpoint kinds:

    PROTOCOL (the default)   PID ABSENT from both records
    APPLICATION_AUTO         01000000  on both
    APPLICATION_EXPLICIT     03000000  on both

Every other vendor PID is byte-identical across all three runs: 0x8000 (07030100 = the 7.3.1.0
product version), 0x8002 (entity virtual GUID), 0x8021 (the compressed TypeObject), and the
reader-only 0x8015. The values agree with the published
DDS_ReliabilityQosPolicyAcknowledgmentModeKind ordering - PROTOCOL 0, APPLICATION_AUTO 1,
APPLICATION_ORDERED 2, APPLICATION_EXPLICIT 3 - so the mapping rests on TWO INDEPENDENT
SOURCES rather than on one observation.

ONE CORRELATED OBSERVATION DELIBERATELY NOT ACTED ON: 0x8009 appears on the SUBSCRIPTION
record ONLY, reading 1 whenever acknowledgment_kind is an APPLICATION kind and 0 under
PROTOCOL. Being reader-only it cannot be the RxO-paired policy, and no published explanation
of it was found, so it is neither emitted nor interpreted. Naming a field we have not
identified is what ADR 0089 section 5 forbids.
